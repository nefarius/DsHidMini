Dual Controller 0.1 is an experimental community extension in headd16/DsHidMini.
It is not an official Nefarius release and does not claim upstream endorsement.
The bridge extension is distributed under the repository BSD-3-Clause license.

The installer bundles unchanged signed upstream components:
- DsHidMini 3.17.1: https://github.com/nefarius/DsHidMini
- BthPS3 3.2.0: https://github.com/nefarius/BthPS3
- ViGEmBus 1.22.0: https://github.com/nefarius/ViGEmBus
- .NET 10 Desktop Runtime 10.0.12: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime
Their original installers and included licenses are retained.
ViGEm is retired; this fork does not claim to maintain the upstream driver.

The bridge embeds Nefarius.ViGEm.Client 1.21.256 (MIT) and the Microsoft .NET
runtime (MIT). .NET license: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime/blob/main/LICENSE.TXT
